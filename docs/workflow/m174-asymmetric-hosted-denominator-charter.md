# M174 Asymmetric Hosted Denominator Charter

## Reporting units

The route contains two separate, unpooled development-only evidence products.
Their metrics, denominators, and interpretations must never be aggregated or
used to estimate a causal card effect.

| Product | Unit and denominator | Reference boundary | Claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main cohort | 30 distinct qualified development cases, all no-card | Q01-derived bounded observations only; no experience card, reference pack, script, path, raw STEP, or retrieval | Terminal behavior of the fixed no-card Harness policy over the frozen cohort |
| Feasibility annex | The existing three direct card-qualified release roles: cylinder/final primitive, block-with-hole/single cut tool, and three-hole-plate/repeated cut tool | Exactly one explicit, hash-bound `vertical-cylinder-construction` card per declared role; no selection, enumeration, or retrieval | Whether the previously released card-assisted closed loop remains executable and attributable for its three roles |

The main cohort's 30 rows must be selected by M175 from the current
registry's declared development population. The annex is not a source of main
cohort rows, not a replacement pool, and not a broader card qualification.

## Repair and completion boundary

The repair surface is unchanged in both products: only an M141-classified
eligible `source_only` failure may receive at most one edit; geometry/semantic,
selector, editability, sandbox/provenance, timeout, protocol, mixed, and
unknown outcomes are terminal. There is no retry or policy adjustment.

The annex retains M170's maximum of four completions per case: Q01, one card,
initial generation, and at most one eligible repair. The no-card main cohort
has the same repair rule but a maximum of three completions per case: Q01,
initial generation, and at most one eligible repair. A missing card is not an
extra generation or repair turn.

## Metrics and controls

Report terminal-gate pass, first-pass, repair eligibility/attempt/conversion/
plateau, failure classes, tool/card compliance where applicable, completion
use, duration, and incomplete/interrupted terminal accounting separately for
each product. The 30-case no-card cohort is the campaign-scale denominator;
the three-case annex is a feasibility/control trace, not a comparative arm.
