# M172 Hosted Milestone Claim and Denominator Charter (Superseded by M174)

> M174 supersedes this equal three-stratum denominator after M173 established
> that the required twenty distinct card-qualified rows do not exist. The
> supported campaign interpretation is now M174's asymmetric denominator;
> this document is retained as historical provenance only.

## Supported claim

For one frozen development-only 30-case campaign, report how a real hosted
model terminates under each of three bounded Harness configurations. Results
are attributable to the declared configuration, frozen cases, tool/card calls,
gates, classified feedback, and completion ceiling.

This is engineering evidence for that fixed campaign. It does not establish
general B-Rep reconstruction, causal card utility, generic repair ability,
held-out readiness, model ranking, or any right to tune after a result.

## Frozen denominator and strata

The reporting population is exactly 30 qualified development cases: ten in
each stratum. A case appears once and only once; no replacement, reassignment,
or post-result exclusion is permitted.

| Stratum | Count | Configuration | Completion ceiling per case |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| S1 | 10 | Q01 only; no card; no repair | Q01 + initial generation |
| S2 | 10 | Q01 plus one explicit, hash-bound card; no repair | Q01 + card + initial generation |
| S3 | 10 | Q01 plus one explicit card, initial generation, gates, classified feedback, and at most one admitted source-only repair | four completions |

The denominator is frozen only after M173 qualifies every case, oracle,
counterexample and explicit no-card/card disposition. M172 selects neither
the cases nor their cards.

## Outcomes and accounting

Report overall and per stratum, with Wilson intervals for terminal-gate and
first-pass rates:

- terminal-gate pass rate and first-pass rate;
- repair eligibility, attempted-repair, conversion, and plateau rates;
- terminal failure-class counts, including all fail-closed categories;
- declared Q01 and card compliance, selected-card hash compliance, and
  completion use versus each per-case and campaign ceiling;
- case duration and stage timing; and
- incomplete/interrupted report status, issued completions, remaining budget,
  and sanitized terminal diagnostics.

S1/S2/S3 are configuration strata, not a causal card comparison: card exposure
and repair availability co-vary between strata. No cross-stratum difference may
be described as a card effect.

## Controls and interpretation

Every row uses the same frozen provider/model declaration, executor, Harness
gates, report schema, deadline policy, and development-only governance. The
no-card configurations are explicit controls. Cases may be compared only
within the limits of their qualified mechanism role and oracle; aggregate
results are descriptive over the frozen denominator.

An invalid tool/card call, hash mismatch, unclassified feedback, non-source
repair, sandbox/provenance/timeout/protocol outcome, or exhausted completion
budget is terminal and retained in the denominator. No retry, case swap,
prompt/model adjustment, policy change, or report-path reuse is allowed.

## Stop rules and downstream boundary

M174 must freeze hashes, provider/model declaration, completion/cost caps,
deadline, executor, report paths, and offline preflight inputs. M175 stops for
fresh hosted preflight, independent review, and itemized user authorization;
it may not issue a request before all three.

If M173 cannot qualify ten cases for a stratum under the stated boundaries,
the route stops as blocked or returns through a newly selected bounded design
workpack. It must not fill the denominator with unreviewed, held-out, or
post-hoc cases.
