## Historical-inventory rule

Every current deferred trigger is classified above. New historical records or
later triggers remain **unclassified** until a separately bounded inventory
review checks their durable authority and re-entry condition. They are not a
prepared queue and may not be inferred to be `future option`s merely because
they exist under `deferred/`.
