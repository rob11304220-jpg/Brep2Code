# Post-M152 Authority and Contract Hardening Route

## Purpose

This route reframes the current post-M146 work as a **project-shaping
hardening route** rather than a capability-expansion or hosted-evaluation
queue. Its primary goal is to fix authority boundaries, implementation
contracts, and maintenance landing zones across development knowledge,
runtime/Harness contracts, and hosted/campaign interpretation. Its secondary
goal is to derive only the smallest additional development-side mappings from
existing reviewed evidence when those mappings are required to keep the
authority/contract picture coherent.

This route is planning authority only. It does not itself authorize runtime
projection, case production, manifest change, provider use, or hosted
execution. `status.md` remains the execution authority.

## Route-wide constraints

- Development-side crosswalks, mappings, theory navigation, and relationship
  layers remain source-linked navigation and maintenance artifacts; they are
  never direct runtime or provider input.
- Implementation-contract mappings may record `implemented`, `contract_only`,
  or `unsupported`; these statuses constrain later route selection and do not
  silently imply capability support.
- Runtime projection and hosted evaluation remain later, separately selected
  routes. Neither may be inferred from a reviewed hypothesis, a completed
  mapping, or a closed documentation milestone.
- Later routes must reuse this route's authority map by linking to it, not by
  copying fields into new registries or route summaries.
