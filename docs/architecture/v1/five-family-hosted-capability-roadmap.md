# Five-Family Hosted Capability Delivery Roadmap

This planning projection operates under the four-track program. It does not select an active workpack, alter a manifest or runtime card, or authorize a provider request. `status.md` remains the execution authority.

## Delivery claim and boundary

For each completed family, the observation-only Harness can produce an independently reconstructed result under its frozen policy and existing execution/gate boundary. This is not a claim of arbitrary B-Rep reconstruction, generic feature-history recovery, or a model benchmark.

| Portfolio family | First bounded question | Existing offline basis | M110 disposition before hosted consideration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prismatic cylindrical cut | Can observed base, radius, axis, centre and extent produce a parameterized through cut? | prismatic-hole and M94--M97 development assets | `inconclusive`: M112 found the unchanged M97 held-out policy cannot distinguish a baseline API failure from a card effect, so it does not admit `TRG-009`. |
| Dependent face selection | Can a unique boss-top face be selected before a blind downstream cut? | `face-selected-dependent-cut-v1` | `not-card-eligible`: frozen dossier links selector/Q02/gates and controls, but grants no runtime-card or provider projection. |
| Multi-inner-loop pocket | Can outer/inner-loop roles produce one blind removed volume with two islands? | `multi-inner-loop-pocket-v1` | `not-card-eligible`: frozen dossier links loop-role/Q02/gates and controls, but grants no runtime-card or provider projection. |
| Repeated feature pattern | Can one fixed four-instance grid preserve count, placement and spacing? | `repeated-feature-pattern-v1` | `not-card-eligible`: frozen dossier links fixed-grid/Q02/gates and controls, but grants no runtime-card or provider projection. |
| Axisymmetric revolve | Can one frozen profile, axis, direction and angle produce an axisymmetric solid? | active six-row governance family under ADR-0064 | `not-card-eligible`: frozen dossier links axis-profile/Q02/gates and controls, but grants no runtime-card or provider projection. |

`fillet`/`chamfer` is a peer mechanism in the CAD taxonomy, but deliberately follows these five as an edge-finishing extension. `shell` or `rib` follows only after it as the selected complex-topology extension.

## Offline acceptance per family

Before a family becomes a hosted candidate, its dossier links, rather than duplicates, these primary records:

1. frozen grammar, family-isolated development/held-out rows, deterministic oracle and hash-stability audit;
2. minimum Q01 facts plus fail-closed missing/ambiguous-fact rejections;
3. one constrained Q02 action, allowed OCP API/import surface and unsupported alternatives;
4. Q03 no-input sandbox, output, geometry, provenance and applicable semantic/editability gates;
5. parameter, sequence and semantic negative controls with a stop rule; and
6. one disposition: no card, candidate-card qualification, counterexample, or no reusable knowledge.

When a family changes case lifecycle or pack/card status, update its primary authority first, then add only the compact relationship to the case portfolio.

## Hosted progression

```text
M134 frozen existing-case development cohort
  -> M135 G3 preflight and itemized user authorization
  -> unchanged serial issuance of 18 independent conditions
  -> record condition terminals; stop only for epoch-integrity faults
  -> M136 independent terminal evidence review
  -> a newly selected later epoch or separate held-out design, if warranted
```

Only one request may be active at a time. M135 owns the frozen epoch policy and
fresh report/monitor paths. A timeout, lifecycle error, script/API failure,
sandbox/provenance result, or failed applicable geometry/semantic gate is a
condition terminal; M135 continues to the next unchanged condition. Only M134
epoch-integrity faults halt issuance. The prismatic-cut question remains
governed by its reviewed successor policy, not by an aggregate success rate.
Fresh preflight and explicit authorization remain required; a route diagram
never supplies either.
