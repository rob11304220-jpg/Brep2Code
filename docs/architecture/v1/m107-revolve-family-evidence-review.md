# M107 Revolve Family Evidence Review

## Evidence reviewed

The six frozen `revolve-v1` records retain a three-centred-axis development /
three-translated-axis held-out family split. The owner reran the family audit,
which verified the exact planar six-segment stepped-radial profile, declared
axis, full 360-degree action, clean-directory normalized STEP stability,
sequence agreement, Q01 facts, geometry, semantic/editability checks and the
wrong-axis, partial-angle, degenerate-profile and split-leak controls.

M106's accepted terminal evidence additionally includes a complete active-case
library replay. During M107, three attempts to repeat that global replay each
stopped in an existing active case when its writer could not create
`output/model.step`. The experimental revolve rows are outside that active
library and the M107 family audit passed; the observation is not attributed to
the family and is not positive evidence.

## Supported boundary

Only one full-revolution, stepped-radial profile in XZ on the positive radial
side of its declared +Z axis is supported. The exact six frozen records may be
proposed for a separately selected, family-scoped lifecycle-promotion package.

## Not supported

The evidence does not establish arbitrary or curved profiles, axes outside the
frozen translated +Z convention, partial/helical or multiple revolutions,
sweep/loft, signed-direction recovery, generic axis/profile recognition,
native history, B-Rep-to-sequence recovery, runtime retrieval, experience
cards, provider use, training input, executable-manifest admission or hosted
capability.
