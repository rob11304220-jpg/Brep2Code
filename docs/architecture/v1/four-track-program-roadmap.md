## Shared rules

- A successful geometry gate alone does not prove reconstruction, parameter
  generalization or feature-history recovery.
- Derived packs may state API/action contracts, observed applicability and
  counterexamples, but cannot include raw STEP, local paths or full reference
  scripts.
- Development and held-out parameter families must be frozen before production
  or model evaluation; a held-out answer cannot be used to revise a card.
- Every hosted workpack has its own hashes, preflight, report paths, deadline,
  request budget and explicit user authorization.

## Efficient operating cadence

The tracks share evidence products and a cadence, but retain independent
technical questions and gates. The products are navigation over existing
authorities, not a new registry, schema, runtime input or authorization:

| Product | Required contents | Primary track use | Authority boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mechanism dossier | Required Q01 facts/rejections, constrained Q02 action, Q03/Q04 gates, counterexamples, linked cases and knowledge units | Reference-assisted and sequence coverage | Links case records and knowledge units; does not make a card or manifest |
| Family design freeze | Grammar, rows/split, oracle, semantic/editability checks, negative controls and stop rule | Modeling-sequence coverage; parameter families | Must precede production; does not create assets or provider scope |
| Family release | Batch production/audit outcome for all frozen rows and their disposition | Modeling-sequence coverage | Variants are one release; it does not promote runtime/hosted use |
| Card qualification dossier | Independent direct evidence, observed applicability, no-card/wrong-card offline controls, card hash and counterexamples | Reference-assisted construction | A reference pack/case card is not itself a runtime card |
| Hosted campaign charter + readiness check | Frozen policy/split/conditions, request accounting, interpretation/stop rules; lifecycle/API/sandbox/provenance/applicable-gate/fresh-path checks | Hosted stability, reference-assisted and parameter variation | Workpack/preflight/authorization remain mandatory; the charter grants no authority |

Use this sequence for a bounded question:

```text
triage one mechanism question
  -> assemble/repair its dossier offline
  -> family design freeze -> family release (when new cases are needed)
  -> card qualification controls (only when a runtime projection is proposed)
  -> hosted campaign charter + readiness
  -> development terminal evidence -> independent decision review
  -> held-out campaign only when separately selected and authorized
```

Do not create a hosted campaign merely because a case has a reference script,
case card or development reference pack. Conversely, do not treat a hosted
pass as a card promotion or general mechanism evidence. Maintain portfolio
links through the [case portfolio](../../corpus/case-portfolio.md) and
[hosted experiment registry](../../workflow/hosted-experiment-registry.md).

### Capacity rule

Keep at most one active hosted campaign across all tracks. It owns its report
and monitor paths until independent terminal review. Offline packages from
different tracks may proceed in parallel only if they do not change the same
case/card/policy authority. A workpack still owns one bounded decision gap;
the cadence reduces duplicate preparation, not evidence or authorization
requirements.

### Deferred-route rule

The M134 cohort is frozen. Until M136 remediation closes, M135 completes a
fresh authorized epoch, and M137 independently reviews its terminal evidence,
all unrelated family campaigns and coverage packages
are deferred. They must not be reintroduced as a default queue merely because
their charters exist.

## Reference-guided parameter-variation route

The M94 rows are a candidate contract, not a hosted-ready evaluation set.  The
following route is the sole planned route for this question; its entries are
backlog packages, not active authorization.

1. **M95 (G2, offline production)** produces only the frozen 3/3 candidate
   rows and audits canonical sequence, semantic/geometry/editability checks,
   clean-directory hash stability, split isolation, and source-leak rejection.
2. **M96 (G2, development-only offline admission)** proves that the bounded
   Q01 observation transcript contains the radius, axis, through-cut and
   x-position facts required by the fixed card, and that fake-provider card
   and no-card paths remain fail-closed and pass unchanged Harness gates.
   Before a held-out row is inspected, it freezes the card/index hashes,
   prompts, CLI policy, scoring and the exact held-out evaluation order.
3. **M97-001 (G3, closed non-passing attempt)** issued three requests, but its
   actual outbound context omitted M96 radius/centre/through facts. The card
   script then failed the OCP API contract and the baseline timed out. It is
   not a card/no-card comparison and its report, paths and capacity cannot be
   reused.
4. **M97-002 (G2, offline remediation)** makes M96 measured facts the actual
   provider-bound context, validates that contract before issuance, and tests
   a supported OCP through-cut recipe plus unsupported-symbol rejection. It is
   development-only and cannot send provider data or alter M94 rows.
5. **M97-003 (G3, complete development calibration)** used the corrected
   observation schema and fresh policy/report paths. Its three card conditions
   passed and two baseline conditions passed; the nominal baseline is a
   generated-script constructor-arity counterexample. It is development-only
   evidence and not a card-effect estimate.
6. **M112 / former TRG-017 (G2, completed `inconclusive`)** found that the
   unchanged policy cannot distinguish a baseline API-use failure from a
   favorable card/no-card asymmetry. It does not admit TRG-009.
7. **M114 and M115 (G2, completed)** chose one successor estimand, registered
   discriminating outcome categories, and froze one reviewed development-only
   policy without inspecting held-out rows or sending a request.
8. **A future newly selected G2/G3 sequence** may next freeze a prismatic
   development campaign charter under the unchanged M115 policy and then—only
   after separate shared hosted-stability gates, preflight and authorization—a
   distinct development or held-out campaign. It cannot reuse M97 or M118
   budget, report, monitor or authorization.
9. **TRG-010 (G2, independent evidence review)** checks split isolation, hashes,
   request accounting, no-input sandbox/provenance, gate outcomes and the
   predeclared card/no-card comparison. It reports only the bounded result;
   it cannot establish general parameter generalization or authorize sampling.

M112 leaves no active held-out route. A later lower-budget feasibility
observation or paired comparison must be newly designed, frozen and selected;
neither can reuse M97 authority or answer a card-effect question by default.
