# ADR-0078: Three-Case Closed-Loop Release Route

- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-08-13

## Context

M139, M140 and M141 separately established campaign preparation, bounded tool
turns and classified fake-provider repair. M19-003 established a one-card,
hash-bound guidance bridge. The project needs an attributable real-provider
closed-loop milestone without turning the case portfolio, repair taxonomy or
card corpus into an open benchmark.

## Decision

Route the milestone through two separately selected triggers: TRG-039 first
freezes an offline G2 release/dossier for exactly `cylinder`,
`block_with_hole` and `three_hole_plate`, each paired with its existing
vertical-cylinder role and M141's one-edit source-only repair policy. TRG-040
may then perform one G3 development-only hosted evaluation of that unchanged
release after fresh preflight and itemized authorization.

## Consequences

The resulting report can distinguish tool/card compliance, initial generation,
gate-stage failure and one eligible source repair while retaining a three-case
denominator. It does not establish generic CAD reconstruction, card utility,
broad retrieval, new repair capability, held-out readiness or authority to
change a frozen campaign in place.
